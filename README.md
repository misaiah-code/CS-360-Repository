# CS-360-Repository

Briefly summarize the requirements and goals of the app you developed. What user needs was this app designed to address?
- For this course, I was tasked with designing and creating a weight tracking app that included a functional log-in screen, a way to track daily weights and see them displayed in a table-like format, a way to set a goal weight, and the option to enable SMS notifications for achieving said goal weight. This app was designed to be a light-weight alternative to some of the more popular and well known health-centric applications.

What screens and features were necessary to support user needs and produce a user-centered UI for the app? How did your UI designs keep users in mind? Why were your designs successful?
- The screens and features were covered in my previous response, but my UI design kept users in mind by maintaining my singular goal of simplicity. I ensured that each and every thing on the screen at any given point had a clear and defined purpose, as well as being spaced so that the application could be approached by any individual from any walk of life. I also ensured that I was consistent with the theme and color screen, and the important aspects were clear highlighted or accentuated. I feel as though my designs were successful due to their readability and clean organization.

How did you approach the process of coding your app? What techniques or strategies did you use? How could those techniques or strategies be applied in the future?
- I approached the coding of my app the same way that I approach most of my coding-based ventures- with patience. I went with one thing at a time and tested frequently. Even if the only thing that I adjusted was an in-line comment, I would test the code again, just to make sure. I feel as though this methodical approach will serve me well for the future.

How did you test to ensure your code was functional? Why is this process important, and what did it reveal?
- For the most part I simply checked with the built-in emulation tools and consistently checked for error or warning messages. This process was important as it enabled me to check if various aspects of my code were functional, as well as allowing me to see the fruits of my efforts in real-time.

Consider the full app design and development process from initial planning to finalization. Where did you have to innovate to overcome a challenge?
- There were a few times during the UI design portion where I had to get a bit creative as things refused to line-up or adjust properly (I blame the infer constraints button). This caused me to make fairly large modifications to my design on a few occasions.

In what specific component of your mobile app were you particularly successful in demonstrating your knowledge, skills, and experience?
- Either the screen navigation options, the SMS notification, or the tables organization and implementation. I am quite proud of my work in all of those segments.
